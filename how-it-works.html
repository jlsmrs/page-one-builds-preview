Five steps, and nothing goes live until you approve it

  1. Answer the intake. The Intake Bot interviews you by audio and digs deep with clarifying questions — you give the rough idea, it asks until the picture is full. It covers what your business does, what to list, how people reach you, and any words or pictures you already have.
  2. We draft your site. The agent builds a five-page draft from your answers — first draft within 2 business days.
  3. You review it. Two revision rounds are included. Change the words, the pictures, the layout — until it sounds like you.
  4. You approve it. Nothing goes live, is published, or is handed off until you say so.
  5. Handoff. The finished files are yours to keep. Help deploying is available as an upsell if you want it.

What the agent does behind the scenes

An agent works behind your build, finding pictures and a background audio track for your site, so five pages feel like a much bigger build.
